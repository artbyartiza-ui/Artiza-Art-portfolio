My process

A clear, collaborative process from first conversation to final files.

01

Project discussion & agreement

We talk through your story and requirements, and sign a clear agreement before work begins.

02

Story understanding

I read your manuscript closely to understand its flow, emotion, and key scenes.

03

Art direction

I define the illustration style, mood, and overall visual approach for your book.

04

Character design

I develop original, story-driven characters that feel engaging and child-friendly.

05

Initial sketches go to you for approval, then I apply color based on your book's theme.

06

Client feedback & revisions

You review the work, and I refine it through a set number of revision rounds.

07

Final delivery

You receive high-quality final illustrations, ready for print or digital publishing.
